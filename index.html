Research

Lock-in before readability: error timing and inference-time compute in text-to-image diffusion

ongoingwith Yuxiao Ye · Qwen-Image, SD2.1 · manuscript in preparation

Swapping the prompt mid-sampling shows that counting and spatial errors lock in within the first 5% of sampling steps, before previews are readable, which is why rolling back to repair them mostly amounts to redrawing the noise. Building on this, I screen out failing samples at the first readable preview (AUC 0.90), and separate the effects of seed, phrasing and model prior: choosing the phrasing before the seed raised accuracy on a pilot set of new spatial prompts from 13.8% to 41.3%.

Video generation evaluation and verifier error attribution

Nov 2026CogVideoX · VBench2 · VQAScore · Qwen3-VL

Best-of-N selection with VQAScore, cross-checked by VBench2, showed that failed generations bias some official benchmark scores. A Qwen3-VL video verifier I built raised false alarms mostly on normal camera motion such as pans and zooms.

Reproducing and auditing DraCo (draft → self-check → refine)

Oct 2026GenEval · GenEval++

Rebuilt the draft–verify–refine pipeline from the paper and released weights, compared self-check, regeneration and local rollback, and cross-checked results with two independent judges.

Rule-grounded sandbox synthesis for financial agents

ongoingwith Chuxue Cao · HKUST

Synthesizing executable sandboxes from real business rules and tool-call trajectories for agent RL and evaluation, by replaying public business-process logs into environments with tools, rules and rule-flip test pairs.

AI Bead Pattern: human-aligned bead pattern generation

open sourcewith Jing Chen

Encodes how people draw fuse-bead art (settle the structure, merge stray colour patches, separate light and shade, trace outlines) as a deterministic generator that matches physical bead palettes by CIEDE2000. Qwen image editing and human review produced 1,162 teacher images in a human cartoon style for later student distillation; GroundingDINO and SAM locate subjects and facial parts to protect key structure.
